A STEM Lab is not just a room with computers; it is an active, collaborative environment where students can touch hardware, build prototypes, make mistakes safely, and iterate on practical solutions.

Key Components of a Modern School STEM Lab

Depending on your institution's grade levels and available space, a SPL Techbot Lab can incorporate:

Student Team Workstations

Reconfigurable collaborative tables configured for group problem-solving, equipped with clean cable channels and safety power points.

Electronics & Circuit Benches

Breadboards, DC bench supplies, digital multimeters, test leads, passive components, and circuit assembly stations.

Microcontrollers & IoT Resources

Development boards (Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi), wireless transmitters, telemetry modules, and sensor shields.

Robotics & Mechanics Inventory

Wheeled rover chassis, high-torque gear motors, precision servo brackets, ultrasonic turrets, and mechanical fasteners.

Component Organization & Storage

Modular color-coded bin racks, lockable safety cabinets for equipment preservation, and dedicated student project lockers.

Demonstration & Testing Arena

Central presentation zone with interactive displays and dedicated open floor arena for rover testing and maze challenges.

Tailored to Your School

Adaptable to Any Grade Level and Campus Layout

Every school has different infrastructure realities. Whether your institution has a dedicated 1,200 sq. ft. room or is repurposing a multi-purpose science classroom, we tailor the setup accordingly.

Primary School Labs (Grades 1–5): Visual block-based logic, non-soldered snap circuits, safe mechanical gears, and interactive kinetic experiments.
Middle School Labs (Grades 6–8): Microcontroller breadboarding, introductory C++ & Python, mobile robotics kits, and multi-sensor automation.
High School Labs (Grades 9–12): Edge AI vision computing, IoT cloud monitoring, autonomous rover pathfinding, and advanced engineering challenges.

Our Implementation Promise

  • Safety Compliant: All electronics use low-voltage DC and non-toxic materials.
  • Zero Hidden Costs: Clear itemized lists of all equipment, furniture, and spare parts.
  • Instructor-Backed: We don't just deliver boxes; our trainers can run the lab.
