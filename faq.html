Everything Schools Need to Know

We believe in total transparency. Here are clear answers to the most common questions raised by school leadership and coordinators.

A STEM lab is a dedicated, hands-on learning environment equipped for practical science, technology, engineering, and mathematics exploration. Unlike a standard computer lab where students primarily interact with screens, a STEM lab provides physical workbenches, microcontrollers, electronics components, robotics chassis, sensors, and testing tools where students build tangible devices and experiment directly with hardware and code.

Our curriculum covers students from Grade 1 through Grade 12 (approx. ages 6 to 18). Each cohort engages with age-appropriate tools: Primary school students start with safe visual block-logic, snap circuits, and kinetic building; Middle school students advance to breadboarding, sensors, and introductory Arduino C++; High school students progress to Python, IoT telemetry, computer vision, and machine learning concepts.

The curriculum covers STEM fundamentals, electronics and circuit building, sensory perception, coding and programming (visual blocks, C++, and Python), mobile robotics, automation, artificial intelligence, IoT, and the engineering design methodology (Understand → Explore → Build → Test → Improve → Innovate).
