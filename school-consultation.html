Structured Methodology

The Six-Step Consultation Process

How we guide your school from initial assessment to ongoing educational growth:

01
Understand

We conduct an initial meeting with school leaders to understand your educational vision, grade structures, and student learning goals.

02
Assess

We evaluate your available campus spaces, power/internet infrastructure, current science labs, and timetable scheduling options.

03
Plan

We design a custom floor layout, student capacity blueprint, hardware bills of materials, and safety guidelines.

04
Recommend

We present tailored equipment selections, kit inventory ratios, grade-mapped curriculum pathways, and trainer staffing requirements.

05
Implement

We manage the lab setup, component organization, resource distribution, and safety testing ahead of student orientation.

06
Support

We provide continuous trainer presence, quarterly curriculum reviews, project mentoring, and competition guidance.

Key Advisory Areas

What We Help Your School Decide

  • Lab Type Selection: Determining whether a general STEM Lab, a dedicated Robotics & AI Lab, or a hybrid multi-purpose maker space is optimal.
  • Hardware & Kit Inventory: Calculating exact kit-to-student ratios, spare component buffers, and durable storage fixtures.
  • Curriculum & Timetable Integration: Mapping hands-on sessions into existing academic periods without overloading school schedules.
  • Trainer & Faculty Model: Determining whether full-time trainer residency, periodic sessions, or faculty co-teaching suits your staff best.
