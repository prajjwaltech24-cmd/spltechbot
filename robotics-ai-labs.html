Where Intelligent Hardware Meets Student Creativity

Our Robotics & AI Lab transforms standard school classrooms into state-of-the-art innovation centers. Here, students don't simply consume modern technology; they unpack how robotic mechanisms move, how sensor feedback loops operate, and how machine learning algorithms perceive the physical world.

To demystify artificial intelligence and robotics through tangible, project-based engineering — equipping students with foundational skills in mechanical design, electrical interfacing, and code architecture.

Curricular Domains

What Students Learn in the Robotics & AI Lab

A comprehensive, progressive curriculum that transitions seamlessly from mechanical fundamentals to autonomous decision-making algorithms.

Mechanical & Kinematic Systems

Understanding chassis structures, torque transmission, gear ratios, linkages, steering mechanisms, and load distribution.

Gear Trains Chassis Geometry Kinematics

Actuators & Motor Control

Programming DC motors, servo angles, stepper precision, and H-bridge drivers using Pulse Width Modulation (PWM).

Servos Stepper Motors PWM Control

Sensory Perception & Telemetry

Interfacing ultrasonic ranging sensors, infrared arrays, line-trackers, gyro sensors, and light-dependent resistors.

Ultrasonic Echo IMU Accelerometers IR Arrays

Microcontrollers & Embedded Coding

Writing C++ and Python logic on microcontrollers (Arduino, ESP32) to read sensor thresholds and control hardware responses.

Arduino C++ MicroPython I2C & SPI

Computer Vision & Image Recognition

Using camera sensors to recognize colors, detect faces, track physical objects, and direct robotic rovers via visual cues.

OpenCV Color Segmentation Object Tracking

Artificial Intelligence & Smart Models

Demystifying machine learning through classification datasets, decision trees, neural network concepts, and voice/gesture control.

Neural Nets Data Training Voice Control
Sample Learning Prototypes

What Students Learn to Build

Real projects built by students as part of our structured robotics and AI progression.

Foundational Project
Smart Ultrasonic Proximity Rover

An autonomous cart that scans continuous distance signals and executes evasive reverse-and-turn maneuvers to avoid walls.

Skills: Digital pin reading, conditional branching, motor driver polarity.
Intermediate Project
High-Precision Line Tracking Rover

A multi-sensor rover using infrared reflectance arrays and closed-loop algorithmic correction to follow intricate road courses.

Skills: Analog thresholding, PID logic, real-time feedback regulation.
Advanced Project
Vision-Guided Object Sorting Arm

A multi-axis robotic arm integrated with an overhead camera that categorizes items by color/shape and stacks them into bins.

Skills: Inverse kinematics, coordinate mapping, computer vision classification.
